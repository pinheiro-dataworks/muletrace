      How to read these cases: the score ranks receiving accounts daily within a budget of 20 alerts per 10,000 accounts. Alerted cases are sampled at evenly spaced ranks across that day's alert list, so false positives appear as often as they do in the queue. Disputed cases are accounts that received an accepted MED-style dispute in the simulated Pix layer; most were not yet active when scored, which is the model's known blind spot. Case files are drafted by the model-free template baseline and passed the deterministic fact checker; no person has reviewed them.
